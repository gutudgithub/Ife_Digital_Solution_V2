# Product scope

## Purpose

Ife Digital Solution replaces paper notebooks and fragmented records with an auditable view
of stock, sales, payments, cash, expenses, and employee activity for Ethiopian small
businesses.

## Phase 1 foundation

The current release establishes:

- users authenticated by email;
- businesses and branches;
- owner, manager, and cashier memberships;
- active-business tenant context;
- clothing and footwear product styles;
- category and size/color variant records;
- business-scoped SKU uniqueness;
- initial dashboard, product list, and product creation;
- translatable interfaces for future English, Amharic, and Afaan Oromoo catalogs.
- immutable purchasing, inventory, sales, return, cash, expense, supplier-settlement, and
  stock-count evidence;
- owner/manager performance intelligence with assigned-cost results, margins, growth,
  accessible time-series graphs, exports, and print output.

Basic online attendance includes branch-scoped check-in, check-out, status, manager
corrections, and audit history.

## Pilot operational release

The completed operational vertical slices cover inventory, sales, returns, cash close,
expenses, supplier settlement, stock reconciliation, attendance, and performance reporting.
Backup, restore, monitoring, support, accountant review, translation review, and
pilot-readiness evidence remain release gates.

## Deferred scope

The following require pilot evidence and separate approval:

- official tax or electronic invoices;
- statutory accounting or VAT filing;
- customer accounts, loyalty, promotions, consent, and public ordering;
- payment-provider integrations;
- multiple active branches;
- delivery workflows;
- professional services and referral marketplaces;
- subscriptions and automated billing;
- offline synchronization;
- AI assistance;
- payroll, wages, leave administration, and statutory employment reporting;
- advanced accounting.

Public business-profile and catalog browsing is proposed separately for Stage 7. That
read-only storefront must not collect customer identity, accept orders or payments, expose
private operational data, or imply that off-platform contact is an Ife transaction.
